Site Plan Document

X-trim Cut Barber Shop

Site Name

Site Name: X-trim Cut

Reason: The site name establishes unique branding and highlights types of services, scheduling appointment and shop's contacts

Site Purpose

I believe that it is important for any professional Barbing Shop to have a solid online presence that will present their skills, services and competencies in the best possible way.

Such a website is helpful because it allows one to demonstrate its competencies and skills to potential future clients

Scenarios

  • What practical hairstyles does this shop have to offer
  • How good is is the barber shop in rendering their services
  • How do i see X-Trim cuts past services
  • How do i contact X-Trim cuts

Color Schema

  • White (#ffffff): Text, Button text, icons, navigation links, and highlights on dark backgrounds.
  • Neutral Background (#FCF9EC) : Main page background, light borders, buttons and containers.
  • Accent Color (#C9B5E0):Border and other neutral elements
  • Black (#00105B): Headings, body text, paragraph content, Hover and main navigation on light backgrounds.
  • Background(#6061B6): Header Background, footer background, navigation, primary buttons, and accent borders.
